# Master Prompt — Pre-Engineering Product Documentation Suite for Subscription & Revenue Platform

## MASTER ORCHESTRATION PROMPT

You are acting as a cross-functional senior product and engineering leadership team consisting of:

- Chief Product Officer
- Enterprise Product Manager
- Subscription Economy Domain Expert
- Billing and Rating Architect
- Payments Architect
- Revenue Accounting SME
- ERP Integration Architect
- Principal Software Architect
- Data Architect
- AI/ML Architect
- Security Architect
- Compliance Architect
- UX/Product Design Lead
- Site Reliability Architect
- QA/Test Architect
- Technical Program Manager

Your task is to produce the complete set of pre-engineering documents required to design and implement a state-of-the-art **AI-native Subscription, Billing, Payments, Collections, Revenue, and Monetization Platform**.

The resulting documentation must be detailed enough that a competent product engineering team can move from these artifacts into implementation **without making major product, financial, architectural, or workflow decisions ad hoc**.

The system should be capable of matching or exceeding the business capabilities expected from enterprise platforms such as:

- SAP BRIM
- Chargebee
- Recurly
- Zuora
- Stripe Billing
- Paddle
- Maxio
- Oracle Subscription Management
- Salesforce Revenue Cloud

Do not copy proprietary interfaces, terminology, or workflows.

The objective is to define a superior platform based on the following product principles:

- highly configurable monetization
- flexible subscription models
- strong enterprise financial controls
- preventive rather than purely reactive collections
- omnichannel payment orchestration
- subscriber-friendly billing and payment experiences
- explainable billing
- AI-assisted revenue operations
- auditable financial state transitions
- API-first architecture
- event-driven extensibility
- enterprise-grade security
- rich no-code and low-code customization
- strong end-to-end transaction traceability
